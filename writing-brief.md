WRITING METHOD
Write each role the way a strong resume writes a job, with the game as the organization.
Build the bullets from the game's iconic projects, duties, and outcomes. Steam achievements are optional evidence. If the source says no achievements were selected, do not mention achievements, and write the iconic work anyway.
Columbia and Emory both teach the same bullet: a varied action verb, what you did and how, then the result. Use four or five bullets. Do not list chores.
Shape of every experience entry:
1. A level-three heading: Role — Organization. Use the role from the source. If none was given, use that game's canonical lead role, such as Heist Lead or Dragonborn, rather than the word Player.
2. The next line is the date range and hours, copied from the source.
3. One plain line, not a bullet, stating the duty of the position. In English it starts with "Responsible for". In Simplified Chinese it starts with "负责". Use the OUTPUT LANGUAGE. Do not leave this line in English when the resume is Chinese.
Do not state how many achievements were unlocked, earned, or completed. Do not write ratios such as "12 of 40" or "已解锁".
4. Four or five bullets. Across them, cover a named project led, a problem that went wrong, the technique or professional skill used, and the outcome.
Bullet formula: [Action verb] [named project or problem] by [skill or technique], [outcome].
One sentence each, two lines at most. Do not start every bullet with the same verb.
Use numbers from the source. A canonical in-game figure, such as a known score or a faction rank, is allowed when it is clearly an in-game result.
Summary, when requested: three sentences on the kind of responsibility, the projects, and the outcomes. No contact details.
Skills, when requested: six to ten short noun phrases a job posting would use, one per bullet, no sentences. Draw them from the bullets. Do not add unrelated tools or languages.
Write the resume in the OUTPUT LANGUAGE. The examples below are patterns, not text to paste.
STYLE EXAMPLES. These are not the applicant. Do not copy a game unless that game is in the source.
Example A, a story game with no role supplied:
### Heist Lead — Grand Theft Auto V
2015-01 – Present · 400 hours
Responsible for planning and running the crew's major scores.
- Planned and executed the Pacific Standard and Union Depository heists by assigning crew roles and timing the entry, securing each job's in-game take.
- Recovered a failed Union Depository approach by changing the entry plan and rerouting the crew after the first route was blocked.
- Coordinated drivers, hackers, and shooters through the live run, holding the timeline when the alarm tripped.
Example B, a named in-game role:
### Dragonborn — The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim
Responsible for ending the dragon crisis and keeping the province intact.
- Led the campaign to slay Alduin, rallying the factions required for the final battle and ending the world-level threat.
- Directed the Thieves Guild through its major jobs, assigning members and restoring the guild's standing in Riften.
- Negotiated the ceasefire at the peace council so both armies would join the fight against Alduin.
Match this specificity. Name the project, name the skill, and name the outcome.
